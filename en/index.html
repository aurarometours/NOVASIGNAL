FAQ

Answers, before you ask.

Can I see something before I pay?

Yes. After the first call we prepare a preview of your homepage design, free and with no obligation. You decide only after you've seen it.

How long until my website is live?

A Signal site in about 3 weeks, a Nova site or online store in 5 to 6 weeks. For apps and software we set milestones together, usually 10 to 16 weeks. Dates are written into the quote.

Will the website really be mine?

Yes. Domain, content and access are in your name. No hidden lock-in: if you ever want to switch providers, you take everything with you.

What happens after launch?

We show you how to update your content and stay reachable for any question. If you'd rather not think about it, the Care plan handles hosting, backups, updates and small edits.

Will my site show up on Google?

Every project ships with technical SEO, speed and a clean structure, so it's ready to be indexed. Climbing competitive searches takes ongoing work: that's what the Orbit plan is for. No serious agency guarantees the #1 spot; we guarantee a method and transparent reports.

You're based in Rome. Can you work with my company?

Yes. We're based in Rome and work remotely with businesses anywhere, in English. Everything happens by phone, WhatsApp or video call, scheduled around your time zone.

Do I need an app, or is a website enough?

It depends on what your customers need to do. On the first call we'll tell you honestly what you really need, even if it's the cheapest option.
