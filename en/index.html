FAQ

Everything you’re wondering about.

How much is a complete site?
Signature is €2,000, all-in. Want to see a free preview of yours first?

What does a site actually cost?
Essential €1,200, Signature €2,000 all-in (or 2 payments of €1,000), bespoke projects from €4,000. No mandatory fees after launch.
How does the free preview work?
We study your business and within a few days show you a real draft of your home page with your content. If it doesn’t convince you, that’s it: no cost, no commitment.
How long until the site is live?
For Signature, 2–3 weeks on average from kick-off, depending on how quickly we gather copy and photos. The preview comes much sooner.
Do I need copy, photos and video ready?
No. We write the copy with you and, if you have no material, use licensed professional photos and video or advise you on producing them.
Do I own the domain and the site?
Yes, always. The domain is registered to you and the site is yours. No lock-in, no mandatory subscription.
How does payment work?
First you see the free preview. If you like it, we start with 50%; the balance is due only on delivery, once the site is live and approved by you.
Do you work outside Italy?
Yes. We work with businesses in Italy, Europe, the UK, the US and the UAE, fully remote, in Italian and English.
What happens after launch?
You get 30 days of included support for changes and tweaks. After that, ask for updates whenever you need them, with no fixed fees.
